I am a physics student at **Zhejiang University** in Hangzhou, China, currently in my third year, and a summer research visitor at the **National University of Singapore**. I work on theoretical and computational condensed matter physics, with a focus on nonlinear transport and dissipation-induced response in quantum materials, and on tools that make long symbolic and numerical derivations verifiable.

## News

- **Oct 2026** Took the finite-Γ nonlinear DC conductivity to real materials: a first-principles chain (VASP → Wannier90 → certified operators → finite-Γ contraction) for MoS₂ and graphene/hBN
- **Sep 2026** Released [Future B](https://github.com/DarrenWongKaWa/future-b), a closed and reproducible methods study of exact-corrected delayed acceptance for first-principles diagrammatic Monte Carlo on LiF polarons
- **Sep 2026** Shipped [symbolic-compactification](https://github.com/DarrenWongKaWa/symbolic-compactification) 0.3 (alpha): an installable skill for verified symbolic reasoning — compactification, paper derivation audits and many-body checks
- **Aug 2026** Drove the *Keldysh4ai* program (Keldysh/NEGF physics-informed AI) past its 560th independently reviewed task closure and wrote the stage closeout for the E10 campaign
- **Aug 2026** Froze the exact finite-Γ compactification of the DC nonlinear response: an analytic one-thermal-master form with nine pointwise-exact minimal generators (channel hierarchy 9 → 3 → 1)
- **Aug 2026** Published the first research note on this site: [Opening the Neural Network to a Physicist](/research/opening-the-neural-network-to-a-physicist/)
